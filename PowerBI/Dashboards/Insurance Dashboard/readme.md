# 📊 Axis Max Life Insurance Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview
This repository contains a **Power BI dashboard** for Axis Max Life Insurance.  
The dashboard provides insights into insurance operations, claims, and customer demographics.  
It enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs, track claim trends, and analyze policy performance across multiple years.

## 📊 Detailed Policy Analysis
The dashboard includes a breakdown of **Travel Insurance policies** across years (2023–2026), segmented by:
- **Claim Status** (Pending, Rejected, Settled)  
- **Gender** (Male/Female)  
- **Premium, Coverage, and Claim Amounts**  

For example:
- In 2025, *Travel – Pending (Female)* policies had a **Premium Amount of 67,874.74**, **Coverage of 45,98,316.85**, and **Claim Amount of 4,05,116.51**.  
- In 2026, *Travel – Settled (Male)* policies recorded **Premiums of 32,932.55**, **Coverage of 22,79,371.41**, and **Claims of 2,21,562.88**.

## 📥 Usage
1. Open `Insurance Dashboard.pdf` to view the exported dashboard.  
2. For interactive exploration, use the `.pbix` file (if available).  
3. Apply filters (Policy Type, Year, Claim Status, Gender) to analyze specific segments.

## 📈 Insights
This dashboard helps:
- Track claim settlement efficiency and rejection rates.  
- Compare performance across policy types (Auto, Health, Travel).  
- Identify trends in customer demographics and claim frequency.  
- Monitor financial metrics like premium vs. claim vs. coverage amounts.
